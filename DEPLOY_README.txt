MEMORA v14 - Netlify-ready package

Upload method:
1. Unzip this package.
2. Open the unzipped folder.
3. Confirm index.html and assets/ are at the first level.
4. Drag the entire unzipped folder into Netlify Deploys.

Correct first-level structure:
index.html
assets/
favicon.svg
icons.svg
_redirects
deploy-check.txt

Do not mix index.html from one version with assets/ from another version.
The QR/result links should be tested after the site has a public https:// URL.
